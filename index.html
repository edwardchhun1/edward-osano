osano. Demo Portal
| Advanced Settings
Frameworks
Support Global Privacy Control (GPC)

When enabled, visitors using a GPC-compliant browser or extension will have their signal automatically applied to their consent settings.

Support Do Not Track

When enabled, visitors using a browser that sends a Do Not Track signal will have it automatically applied to their consent settings.

IAB GPP & EU TCF 2.x Frameworks

When enabled, Osano CMP will implement the IAB Global Privacy Platform and IAB EU TCF 2.x Frameworks along with their respective APIs.

IAB US Privacy String Notice Confirmation

By enabling this toggle, Customer confirms they have provided consumer notice and Do Not Sell or Share links under applicable regulations.

Google Consent Mode

When enabled, Google Consent Mode for Google Analytics is supported. GCM must be implemented via your Google configuration for this option to function.

Amazon Consent

When enabled, Amazon Consent Signal will be shared with Amazon Ads scripts on your site.

Microsoft UET Consent Mode

When enabled, Osano will share user consent choices with Microsoft UET. Note: incompatible with IAB TCF 2.x consent signals.

Performance
Split Payload

When enabled, osano.js is segmented into a reduced initial payload and additional requests are made as needed. May improve site render performance.

Experience
Curated Block List

When enabled, Osano automatically blocks a curated list of scripts and cookies in regions where their usage has been declared illegal.

Add Consent UUID to Preference Drawer

When enabled, the end user's consent UUID will be present in the Preference Drawer with a copy button.

First Layer Categories

When enabled, users in EEA member countries and the UK are shown cookie category toggles directly in the banner. When disabled, category toggles are only in the preference drawer.

Add Manage Preferences Button

When enabled, a Manage Preferences button will be present on banner templates 1 and 3.

US State Level Legislation Opt-Out Banner Format

When enabled, visitors in states with active privacy legislation will be shown banner format 1 (implied consent/opt-out). When disabled, banner format 3 is shown (explicit opt-in required).

Cross Domain Support

When enabled, user consent from one domain applies to other distinct domains added to the same configuration.

Delete Storage on Category Opt-Out

When enabled, cookies and local storage items belonging to a consent category will be automatically deleted when a user withdraws consent. Warning: may break third-party functionality.
