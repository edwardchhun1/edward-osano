osano. Demo Portal
| Advanced Settings
Frameworks
Support Global Privacy Control (GPC)

Visitors using a GPC-compliant browser or extension will have their signal automatically applied to their consent settings. When disabled, GPC signals will not be honored.

Support Do Not Track

Visitors using a browser that sends a Do Not Track signal will have it automatically applied to their consent settings. When disabled, DNT signals will not be honored.

IAB GPP & EU TCF 2.x Frameworks

When enabled, Osano CMP will implement the IAB Global Privacy Platform and IAB EU TCF 2.x Frameworks along with their respective APIs.

IAB US Privacy String Notice Confirmation

By enabling this toggle, Customer confirms they have provided consumer notice and Do Not Sell or Share links under applicable regulations and laws.

Google Consent Mode

Google Consent Mode for Google Analytics is supported. GCM must be implemented via your Google configuration for this option to function.

Amazon Consent

Amazon Consent Signal will be shared with Amazon Ads scripts on your site. If you're not using Amazon Ads scripts, leave this off.

Microsoft UET Consent Mode

Osano will share user consent choices with Microsoft UET. Note: incompatible with IAB TCF 2.x consent signals.

Performance
Split Payload

osano.js is segmented into a reduced initial payload; additional requests are made as needed. This may help your site render more efficiently.

Experience
Curated Block List

Osano automatically blocks a curated list of scripts and cookies in regions where their specific usage has been declared illegal by regulatory bodies.

Add Consent UUID to Preference Drawer

The end user's consent UUID will be present in the Preference Drawer with a copy button. When disabled, users must access their Osano cookie in the browser.

First Layer Categories

Users in EEA member countries and the UK are shown cookie category toggles in the banner. When disabled, category toggles only appear in the preference drawer.

Add Manage Preferences Button

A Manage Preferences button will be present on banner templates 1 and 3. When disabled, these templates will have no Manage Preferences button.

US State Level Legislation Opt-Out Banner Format

Visitors in states with active privacy legislation are shown banner format 1 (implied consent). When disabled, banner format 3 is shown (explicit opt-in required).

Cross Domain Support

User consent from one domain applies to other distinct domains in the same configuration. When disabled, consent only applies to the single domain.

Delete Storage on Category Opt-Out

Cookies and local storage items belonging to a consent category will be automatically deleted when a user withdraws consent. Warning: may break third-party functionality.
